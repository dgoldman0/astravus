## Sprites and keys

The five parents now have separate multiview keys. Arin and Sage receive the opening identity corrections in their sprites; their native masks preserve lower bodies and feet. Maia's gardening/home pair and Dorian retain appropriate adult construction and modest childhood-era silver. Selene retains her vital adult face and genetic white hair. The keys include warm-interior and cool-shade examples rather than prescribing one universal skin or iris RGB value.

All six supporting humans have keys. Kael remains a lean older child with dark tousled hair and hazel eyes, without later injuries. Lyra stays smaller, curly-haired and freckled; a bounded native eye deformation retains her sprite's smile and existing prop. Thalia's green iris correction preserves pupils, highlights and face shape. Lyron's new garment removes the rejected modern shirt/cargo silhouette while maintaining his human face. Soren's focused face and practical work pose, and Kaleb's angular weathered face and restrained expression, are retained. Their source notes distinguish explicit literary facts from inferred contours and costumes.

Cali, Cassia and Joren retain all fourteen existing human sprite variants, including home, festival, frustration, mourning and painting states where supplied. Six new keys document their two childhood stages. The later figures have longer limbs and less rounded faces; they remain children. Sheet errors in Cali's viewing angles and Cassia's overly geometric birthmark were corrected before selection. A character key is not an instruction to use one outfit or facial expression in every scene.

Shadow, Barkley and Nibble retain their three sprite images. Their keys preserve species/build differences and the rat < cat < dog size order. Shadow's anatomical-left ear notch and crooked tail tip remain recognizable. Nibble's anatomical-right violet and left coral eye map to viewer-left violet/right coral in front view; her profile mapping was corrected before selection. Her fuller black-and-white coat follows the supplied author design. Barkley's golden coat and retriever build remain ordinary canine anatomy at this stage.

The selected sheets provide useful body views, head angles and expressions. Some front-oriented body poses retain a slight turn, and unseen back construction is inferred; they are not engineering orthographic drawings. Source identity, stage, scene performance and native in-game framing take precedence over literal copying of a sheet pose.
