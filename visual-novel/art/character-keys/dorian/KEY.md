## Stage, clothes and performance

First Breath uses the cream/tan shirt. Everyday childhood scenes add the long teal sleeveless coat, brown trousers and boots. The notebook belongs in his anatomical left hand: viewer's right in a front view, viewer's left in a rear view. The sheet follows that relationship; do not mirror the notebook just to preserve screen position.

The biography's mostly silver beard, accumulated hunch and “125+” concern undated later-life detail. Keep this adult vital and broad-shouldered with only modest visual aging from the opening. Current small gray accents suffice; a mostly white beard or pronounced stoop would imply a different stage.

Warmth lifts his mouth and eyes; attention holds a steady listening gaze; concern gathers the brows. Narration can be animated and expansive without making every resting pose theatrical. Warm light adds gold highlights while retaining dark skin depth. Cool shade keeps brown irises and readable facial midtones beneath the glasses.
