Keep the same small notch on that left ear when it turns. A page-left-facing side view exposes her anatomical left side, so the near ear is notched. Her other ear stays intact. There is exactly one slender tail attached at the rump, ending in one small crooked upward tip. Do not create a second tail through an overlapping loop, a bushy tail, extra paws or a detached tail curve.

Her coat is black with charcoal and warm reflected highlights. Green irises can catch low light as the prose describes, but must not become neon beams. Preserve the selected narrow muzzle, ear proportions and graceful domestic-cat body; do not replace her with a kitten, another breed or a heavier animal.

## Scale, mood and light

Shadow is smaller than Barkley and much larger than Nibble. The key's “Rat < Cat < Dog” note is a size ordering, not a numerical measurement. A seated cat and a standing dog cannot be compared simply by top-of-head height; account for pose and depth.

Her mood comes through alert ears, small head turns and a quiet gaze. Human smiles, expressive eyebrows or theatrical grief would change the animal design. Use diffuse light and restrained fill so black fur remains readable without turning brown or gray. The selected runtime sprite's blue backing is technical compositing material, not her lighting or fur color.
