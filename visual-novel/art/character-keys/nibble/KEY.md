# Nibble: familiar visual key

[Visual key](key.png)

Nibble follows the author's selected fluffy rat design. The key shows that same character through sitting front, standing left profile, rear view, facial angles and an eye-color detail. It does not replace her deliberate stylization with a generic laboratory rat or mouse.

## Authority and identity

[Book I](../../../../revision/latest.md) and [familiar lore](../../../../wiki/worldbuilding/Familiars.md) establish a small inquisitive rat who can perch on a child's shoulder. The author's September 4 visual design, retained in the selected [sprite](../../../game/images/familiars/nibble.png) and documented in [familiar-assets.json](../../../docs/familiar-assets.json), establishes her actual coat, ears, face and mismatched eyes.

Preserve fluffy black/brown-and-white fur, the broad tousled white forehead blaze, white muzzle/chest, dark eye and shoulder patches, large pink ears, pink paws, fine whiskers and one long bare pink tail. Rear haunches remain white rather than acquiring additional dark spots. Unseen surfaces continue the selected distribution without inventing a new coat variation.

Her anatomical **right eye is violet; left eye is coral-red**. In a frontal view this means violet on the viewer's left and coral on the viewer's right. A page-left-facing strict profile exposes her left side and must show the coral eye. A page-right-facing strict profile exposes her right side and must show violet. Do not mirror the whole design when changing her facing direction.

## Scale, anatomy, mood and light

Nibble is much smaller than Shadow, who is smaller than Barkley. She can perch on a child's shoulder and should not become cat-sized because her key fills a page. No exact measurements are introduced.

The large expressive eyes, large ears and full coat are intentional author choices. Preserve them while maintaining a rat's elongated body, small articulated paws and tapering bare tail. Do not shrink her eyes to ordinary rodent size, replace her coat with agouti brown, create a hamster tail, or enlarge her proportions further. Curiosity comes from her head tilt and alert posture, not human smiles or clothing.

Use soft neutral daylight with clear white-fur detail and gentle warm dark-fur highlights. Eye pigment remains violet/coral across lighting and angle. It should not flood the surrounding face with colored light. The selected green matte is a compositing backing and does not belong in the white fur.

## Reference use

Keep the eye assignment physically consistent through every view and preserve the white rear haunches. The selected runtime sprite retains the author-supplied identity reference.
