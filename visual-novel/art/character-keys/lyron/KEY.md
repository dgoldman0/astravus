# Lyron visual key

[View key](key.png). Reference for Lyron's gentle adult identity and everyday Lumen wardrobe during Book I.

## Source and stage

[Book I](../../../../revision/latest.md), lines 355–361, establishes a tall, gentle agricultural systems manager with salt-and-pepper hair and kind hazel eyes. [Cassia's biography](../../../../wiki/bios/Cassia.md), the Lyron section, confirms his practical role and warmth. He is shown during Cali's childhood; no exact age or elderly stage is inferred from gray hair.

Lumen already blends nature and subtle technology in [Book I](../../../../revision/latest.md), lines 57 and 69. [Technology](../../../../wiki/worldbuilding/Technology.md), fundamental principles, describes organic integration and responsive systems. [Socioeconomics](../../../../wiki/worldbuilding/Socioeconomics.md), Textile Weavers and Automation sections, establishes textile craft alongside automated routine production. Theron's natural fibers and subtly light-responsive material at manuscript line 565 provide a material precedent, not a requirement that Lyron wear an elder's robes.

## Keep consistent

Preserve warm medium-brown skin, a gentle long oval face, softer jaw corners, straight slightly longer nose, relaxed brows, kind hazel eyes, close salt-and-pepper waves and a neat short beard. These precise facial contours and complexion are production choices anchored in the existing sprite. They distinguish Lyron from Kaleb's more angular, rugged construction while preserving recognizability.

## Current wardrobe

The selected outfit is a softly structured blue-gray overlapping garment ending around the upper thigh, with curving panels, unobtrusive integrated side closure, inset seam storage, comfortable three-quarter sleeves and a divided hem. Plain relaxed earth-taupe trousers and quiet woven shoes complete it. The fine natural-fiber texture has tonal depth across its planes.

The exact cut, closure and material behavior are selected production interpretations. They do not establish a Lumen uniform, a guild badge, ubiquitous adaptive clothing or a new technology.

## Performance and light

Warm small smile, calm explanation and thoughtful concern should share the same facial construction. Keep his manner unhurried and capable. Do not turn gray hair into frailty or dress him as a generic sage.

Preserve hazel eyes and warm brown skin under differing illumination; the cloth is tactile and non-luminous. Front-oriented, side and back garment views agree on the overlap and divided hem.
