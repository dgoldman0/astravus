# Sage — visual key

[Open the visual sheet](key.png). The 1536 × 1024 sheet supplies full-body front, side and back studies; neutral front, three-quarter and profile heads; restrained warmth, attention and concern; materials and lighting examples. It is a practical continuity sheet, not a set of exact anatomical measurements.

## Identity and sources

[The childhood manuscript](../../../../revision/latest.md#earliest-memories), line 39, establishes sandy short hair, freckles and a calm, nurturing presence. [Sage's biography](../../../../wiki/bios/Sage.md#physical-description) explicitly adds round soft features, warm tan skin with rosy undertones, gray eyes, heavy freckles and medium height/build. Those source traits govern this refinement.

The far-right adult in the [accepted opening](../../character-refinement-test/opening-refined-v1.png) is the face/build anchor: fuller cheeks, gently rounded jaw and chin, a soft substantial neck and swept sandy crop. Sage remains an androgynous adult. They should not acquire Arin's pointed jaw, hollow cheeks, pale complexion or auburn hair. Preserve gray irises without making them icy or luminous.

## Stage, clothes and performance

First Breath uses cream sleeves and the blue-teal waistcoat. Childhood indoor scenes use the olive-green tunic, russet trousers, small layered necklaces and bare feet. Bare feet are a documented indoor sensory preference, not an obligation in every location. The everyday crossed-hand gesture is quiet listening; an embrace or holding Lyra needs a different action pose.

The biography's “125+” is later narrative context. This pass establishes no numerical age at Calista's birth. Keep a soft vital adult face through Book I, allowing modest continuity aging without recreating the former gaunt sprite. A medium build is neither a narrow model figure nor exaggerated heaviness.

Warmth shows in relaxed eyes and a small smile; attention listens directly; concern is compassionate and restrained. They need not smile during grief. Warm light brings out rosy-gold skin, while cool shade preserves warm tan midtones. The face and sandy hair should remain distinguishable from Arin even when iris color is hidden.

## Review and use

The new sheet corrects the older sprite's narrow face and builds a consistent rounded identity across views. The everyday sprite is refined with generated paint and editable GIMP masks for the head and clothed upper body. Its crossed hands, lower trousers and bare-foot placement are retained from the original.
