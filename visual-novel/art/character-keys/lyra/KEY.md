# Lyra visual key

[View key](key.png). Selected reference for the small childhood Lyra seen in Book I.

## Source and stage

[Book I](../../../../revision/latest.md), line 49, describes a small, sprightly child with golden curls and wide green eyes. The [biography](../../../../wiki/bios/Lyra.md), physical description, adds golden-tan skin with olive undertones, golden freckles, a button nose, subtle pointed ears and a front-tooth gap. Keep her younger and smaller than Cali; the sheet does not establish an exact age or age gap.

## Keep consistent

Golden-blonde unruly curls, small hair clips, golden-tan freckled skin, green irises, round youthful cheeks, button nose and small stature establish her identity. Wide curiosity comes from gaze, brows and posture. Eye apertures and irises should remain natural child proportions, with modest catchlights and modeled lids. Do not lengthen her face, make her pale peach, turn her hair chestnut or age her into a teenager.

## Wardrobe and performance

Keep the russet shirt, muted olive overalls and russet boots. Those wardrobe details and the back construction are production choices. Empty-handed key views show the garment. The standing sprite holds a feather; use that prop only when the action calls for it.

The expression row covers delighted laughter, inquisitive listening, and frightened tearfulness. The neutral face is quiet; it should not make an active scene look sleepy. For the pond, preserve fear and the later aftershock, wet hair/clothes and the siblings' supporting contact. Use the tooth gap when she actually opens her mouth to laugh; no permanent tooth display.

## Light and reference use

Keep green eyes within their natural dark pupil and sclera, without emitting light. Golden skin and freckles must survive both cool fill and warm reflected light. Hair highlights remain golden, not orange-white.

Use the face angles and expressions while preserving her youthful proportions and scene-specific gaze. Exact back seams and subtle ear visibility remain production interpretations.
