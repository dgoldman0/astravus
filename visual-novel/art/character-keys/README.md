The [manuscript](../../../revision/latest.md), established wiki details and author clarifications govern identity and chronology. Read the character's `KEY.md` before using a sheet: it distinguishes source facts from inferred profiles, costume construction and other production choices. The collection covers the depicted Book I cast, not future-book characters or incidental crowds.

Both friendship stages remain childhood. Choose clothing and expression for the activity, mood and continuous scene. Younger Astraviin can look naturally human; this does not assign exact adult ages or move undated later-life changes into Book I.

Sheets fill their pages independently and are practical drawing references, not measured orthographic plans. Use [character continuity](../../docs/CHARACTER_CONTINUITY.md) for relative stature and scene staging. Keep the familiar size order: rat smaller than cat smaller than dog. Match scene lighting while preserving complexion, iris pigment and recognizable face construction.

This folder holds selected sheets and working reference notes. Prompts, earlier candidates, editable files, review evidence and decision history live in [character development records](../../../development/visual-novel/art/characters/README.md).

[Catalog data](catalog.json) and the embedded `catalog-data` in `index.html` must be updated together when a selected sheet or reference changes.
