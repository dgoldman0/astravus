# Kaleb visual key

[View key](key.png). Selected production reference for Kaleb as an adult during the childhood adventures and Joren's memorial.

## Source and stage

[Book I](../../../../revision/latest.md), lines 375–377, specifies a tall rugged explorer, weathered face, brown hair streaked with gray and warm golden-brown eyes. At line 501 his memorial address is steady but emotional. [Joren's biography](../../../../wiki/bios/Joren.md), the Kaleb section, confirms the appearance. Do not equate gray streaks with a frail elderly stage or establish an exact age.

## Keep consistent

Retain the angular jaw and cheek structure, strong expressive brow, irregular brown waves with gray streaks, short rough beard and warm golden-brown eyes. Complexion, beard arrangement and exact facial geometry are production choices from the existing sprite. Compared with Lyron, preserve the more angular rugged construction and irregular hair silhouette.

## Wardrobe and performance

Keep the layered brown travel coat, charcoal/teal underlayers, muted scarf, belt and worn boots. Practical layers and their back construction are a production interpretation, not a prescribed explorer uniform. Add no weapons, armor or unsupported scars.

The key includes an engaged adventurous smile, attentive seriousness and restrained grief. His source warmth should remain available in ordinary exploration; his memorial face should not be a grin. Those performances must preserve the same nose, brow, jaw and age.

## Light and reference use

Golden-brown irises should not become yellow lamps or green hazel. Retain warm skin midtones, brown hair between gray strands and readable fabric depth under subdued lighting.

The sheet provides facial angles and an emotional range alongside the full body views. It does not prescribe clothing for every trip or later age.
