# Cassia: childhood visual key

[Early key](early-key.png) · [Later key](later-key.png)

Both sheets depict Book I childhood: first friendship and later shared projects. Neither depicts the adult author or Book II's late-teen period. The full-body front, side and back views establish the selected costume and silhouette; facial views show structure, attention and a limited natural emotional range.

## Authority and identity

[Book I's first friendship](../../../../revision/latest.md) describes a slender child with long, wavy chestnut hair, deep green eyes and lively storytelling. [Cassia's biography](../../../../wiki/bios/Cassia.md) adds rich umber skin and a vaguely crescent-shaped left-wrist birthmark. The [young](../../../game/images/characters/book-one/cassia-young.png), [older](../../../game/images/characters/book-one/cassia-older.png) and [mourning](../../../game/images/characters/book-one/cassia-mourning.png) sprites are the selected identity and shading references.

Keep her complexion distinctly umber, her eyes deep green and her long waves/curls chestnut. Preserve the author-approved facial midtones, gentle cheek/nose/forehead modeling and softened lip boundary. A harsh dark mouth outline, crushed facial shadows or a lighter complexion would undo that correction. Keep her eye aperture tied to the selected face, without enlarging the irises or making every expression a fixed smile.

The small birthmark is natural, irregular, low-contrast skin pigmentation on her anatomical left wrist. It is not a perfect gold crescent, tattoo, jewelry or magical mark. Her biography does not date the childhood nose injury relative to these scenes, so the keys preserve the selected nose without introducing a new injury stage.

## Body and wardrobe

Early clothes retain the mustard headband/scarf, plum tunic, slate-blue loose trousers, cream cuffs, boots, satchel and small story notebook. Later clothes retain the plum tunic, olive vest, rusty-brown trousers, ochre hair tie and boots. Back seams and obscured costume surfaces continue the selected construction. These details are production design rather than new literary facts.

Cassia remains slender, with modestly longer limbs and a less rounded face in later childhood. At a shared standing depth she is nearly Cali's height, with Joren slightly taller: existing runtime framing is 680 versus Cali's 675 and Joren's 710 stage pixels early, then 755 versus 750 and 790 later. These are composition values, not physical measurements. Page-filling drawings are not an inter-sheet scale chart.

## Performance and light

Early expressions retain curiosity, storytelling warmth, thought and slight worry. The later sheet adds frustration and restrained sadness. Its sadness portrait includes the selected mourning shawl as a specific wardrobe reference; the turnaround remains in exploration clothes. Do not carry that shawl or grief performance into ordinary earlier play.

Use soft neutral daylight with warm highlights and enough fill to distinguish umber facial planes. Warm scene light may alter reflected highlights but must not erase her green irises or make her face a flat dark shape. Avoid borrowing Calista's olive skin, shorter facial construction or blue eyes.
