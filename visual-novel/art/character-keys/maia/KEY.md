# Maia — visual key

[Open the visual sheet](key.png). ## Identity and sources

[The childhood manuscript](../../../../revision/latest.md#earliest-memories), especially line 25, establishes a tall, slender woman, warm brown skin, and long silver-streaked dark hair braided with seeds or dried flowers. [Maia's biography](../../../../wiki/bios/Maia.md#physical-description) adds curls, small braids and rich amber eyes flecked green. Preserve the existing oval face, gentle mouth and distinct amber-green gaze. Silver strands run through dark hair; they must not turn into a white-haired design resembling Selene.

The front home sprite, gardening sprite and center parent holding Calista in the [accepted opening](../../character-refinement-test/opening-refined-v1.png) anchor this sheet. Her many narrow braids remain integrated with loose curls. Small botanical ornaments are personal objects, not a uniform or new cultural symbol.

## Stage, clothes and performance

First Breath shows the same adult in cream clothing holding newborn Calista. Childhood home scenes use the blue-teal blouse, ochre wrap vest, russet trousers and soft brown shoes. Gardening uses the existing copper-brown apron, cream sleeves, teal trousers and boots. The seed in the gardening sprite belongs to that lesson; the home pose has empty hands.

Keep a vital adult face through Book I, with modest silver already present. The biography's “125+” concerns the later narrative. Do not assign the permanent soil stains, palm scar or decades of accumulated wear a new childhood date. An action illustration may show ordinary soil from the present gardening activity.

Warmth softens the mouth and eyes; concern lifts the inner brows slightly without making her look aged or distressed in every scene. Her posture is patient, attentive and comfortable handling plants. Warm lamplight may add gold highlights, while cool shade retains warm brown midtones and amber-green irises. Avoid flattening her skin into orange or gray.
