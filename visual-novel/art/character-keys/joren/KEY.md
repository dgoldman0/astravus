# Joren: childhood visual key

[Early key](early-key.png) · [Later key](later-key.png)

These keys follow the living childhood friend through early adventures and later Book I projects. “Later” still means childhood, never a late-teen or adult Joren. The sadness study is ordinary living-child disappointment; it does not depict him after his death or authorize a new post-death appearance.

## Authority and identity

[Book I's first meeting and projects](../../../../revision/latest.md) establish a sturdy boy with tousled blond hair, piercing blue eyes and energetic curiosity. [Joren's biography](../../../../wiki/bios/Joren.md) adds fair ruddy skin, a small front-tooth gap and perpetually healing knee scrapes. The selected [young](../../../game/images/characters/book-one/joren-young.png), [older](../../../game/images/characters/book-one/joren-older.png) and [frustrated](../../../game/images/characters/book-one/joren-frustrated.png) sprites anchor the actual identity and performance.

Preserve his broad boyish face, lively brows, fair complexion that flushes easily, blond hair silhouette and blue irises. The tooth gap is small and appears only when the mouth naturally exposes the front teeth. Do not draw teeth through closed lips. Exposed early knees can show little healing scrapes without adding a new injury event.

## Proportion and clothing

Joren is sturdier and slightly taller than the girls at a shared standing depth. Existing runtime framing is 710 stage pixels early and 790 later, compared with Cali's 675/750 and Cassia's 680/755. These values guide scene composition and are not physical heights. The separate sheets fill their own canvases; do not infer group scale from page size.

The early key retains a rust-orange short-sleeve overshirt, cream henley, patched dark shorts, scrunched socks, brown boots and explorer satchel. Later clothes remain a slate-teal overshirt, rust henley, patched brown long trousers, boots and satchel. The small brass device inset copies the selected later prop rather than introducing a new mechanism or lore. Turnaround hands are empty so the construction remains legible.

Longer later-childhood limbs and modestly longer facial structure must not become adult shoulders, a narrow adolescent body or a different boy. Do not simply scale the early sprite taller or put the early shorts back into a later continuous scene.

## Performance and light

Warm, energetic amusement is balanced with attentive, thinking, frustrated and quiet expressions. The frustrated study follows the selected brow and mouth tension without distorting the face. His smile is not appropriate to every danger, argument or loss-related recollection.

Keep softly dimensional painterly skin and blond highlights under diffuse neutral light. Ruddy cheeks are a local complexion trait rather than an orange wash across the whole character. His blue eyes remain embedded, reflective eyes without added glow. Keep him visually separate from Calista's chestnut/olive palette and Cassia's chestnut/umber/green palette.
