# Kael visual key

[View key](key.png). Selected production reference for Kael during the early family and childhood exploration scenes. This sheet is a design aid, not a new canonical biography or a measurement drawing.

## Source and stage

[Book I](../../../../revision/latest.md) describes Kael at line 45 as Cali's older brother, tall for his age and lean, with tousled dark hair and hazel eyes. The [biography](../../../../wiki/bios/Kael.md), physical description, adds deep warm brown skin and gold-dominant hazel irises. Its mature athletic bulk and undated injuries are not applied to this childhood stage. No exact age or age gap is established by the sheet.

## Keep consistent

Preserve the long-limbed lean child, dark tousled curls, warm brown complexion, gold-hazel irises, small natural nose and familiar mischievous expression. Keep him visibly older and taller than young Cali and Lyra without making him a small adult. Do not introduce the biography's eyebrow scar, broken nose, adult shoulder mass or beard. The biography places a grin dimple on his anatomical left cheek; painted smile creases in this sheet are not a reliable left/right locator, so check that detail in future close shots.

## Wardrobe and performance

Retain the brown overshirt, beige undershirt, earth trousers, collector pouch and boots. Clothing cut, pockets and the inferred back view are production choices. The neutral turnarounds use empty hands; the existing scene sprite still holds its small collected stone. A held prop must follow the action.

The face row separates a mischievous grin, intent listening and worried concern. Keep the same jaw, nose, eye scale and age when changing expression. Strong excitement can move the whole body; ordinary listening should not turn into a fixed grin.

## Light and review

The sheet uses softly warm neutral studio light. Preserve underlying brown skin in cooler scenes rather than whitening it, and keep gold-hazel irises readable without yellow glow. Shadow color is not skin color. The front-oriented, side and back views preserve the same outfit and stature; they are illustrative views, not exact orthographic anatomy.

The sprite already matches the intended childhood design and is retained. The full key gives additional angles and expression direction. Other age stages and the exact dimple placement still need shot-specific checks. [Prompt](prompt-key.txt); generation and source hashes are in [supporting changes](../supporting-changes.json).
