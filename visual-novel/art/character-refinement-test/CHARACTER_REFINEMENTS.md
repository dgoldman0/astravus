# Character refinement directions

These are production proposals grounded in the current manuscript and wiki, prepared for the September 8, 2026 opening-scene test. They do not establish new canon or constitute approved multi-view character keys. Only Arin and Sage are visually revised in this test; the other entries define what to preserve and what a later refinement should address.

## Source and stage

[Book I's opening](../../../revision/latest.md) establishes the parents' recollection of Calista's First Breath and subsequent childhood. The biographies supply additional physical details. Their later-life age headings and accumulated injuries do not automatically apply at Calista's birth. The existing [stage guidance](../../docs/CHARACTER_CONTINUITY.md) remains useful: five earlier-life adult parents and one newborn in this composition, with childhood siblings introduced later.

Use the written trait as the requirement and the proposed shape or performance as an interpretation. A precise jaw contour, haircut arrangement, costume palette or posture is a production choice where the prose does not specify it. Preserve natural downward gazes in the opening; iris colors should not require turning the cast toward the camera.

## The five parents

### Arin

**Source:** `latest.md:29` specifies cropped auburn hair, sharp blue eyes and a lean, muscular build. [Arin's biography](../../../wiki/bios/Arin.md) adds pale freckled skin, strong forearms and average stature below Maia and Dorian.

**Refined direction:** preserve the existing defined profile, lean silhouette and capable hands. Use a genuinely cropped auburn silhouette with close sides and nape and a short textured top. Pale skin should retain warm reflected light and visible freckles while remaining distinct from Sage's tan complexion. Focused attention can coexist with the tenderness of this scene.

**Test change:** tighten the crop and adjust exposed skin consistently across face, neck, hands and forearms. Keep the established facial structure, clothing and pose. Do not add a missing fingertip or invent the timing of scars. Workshop smudges belong in suitable working scenes, not as an identifying prop in every appearance.

### Sage

**Source:** `latest.md:39` gives short sandy hair, freckles and a gentle presence. [Sage's biography](../../../wiki/bios/Sage.md) explicitly establishes round, soft features, warm tan skin with rosy undertones, gray eyes, heavy freckles and a medium build.

**Refined direction:** fuller cheek volume, a softly curved jaw and rounded chin should give Sage a distinct face at the same age and angle as Arin. A calmer, gently wavy short sandy crop supplies a separate silhouette. The neck and clothed upper body should read as a natural medium build. Preserve their adult androgynous appearance and quiet attention; fullness is not a reason to exaggerate the body or infantilize the face.

**Test change:** revise face construction, hair silhouette and modest upper-body fullness. Preserve the seated pose, inward gaze, existing clothes, jewelry and hand placement. This is the strongest source correction: the original narrow jaw and lean neck did not convincingly embody the specified softness and medium build.

### Maia

**Source:** `latest.md:25` specifies tall, slender stature, warm brown skin and silver-streaked dark hair in elaborate braids with seeds or dried flowers. [Maia's biography](../../../wiki/bios/Maia.md) adds curls, small braids and amber eyes flecked green.

**Refined direction:** retain her recognizable face, warm complexion and interwoven curls/braids. A future childhood key should show clearly identifiable silver strands without turning her into her later-life self. Keep her silhouette long and slender and her hands expressive.

**Opening decision:** preserve the existing central figure and all baby-supporting hands exactly. The chronology does not require importing every later childhood or lifelong work mark into First Breath. Visible silver in the childhood sprites can be evaluated separately from this controlled Arin–Sage trial.

### Selene

**Source:** `latest.md:33` establishes flowing white hair, violet eyes and long, delicate fingers. [Selene's biography](../../../wiki/bios/Selene.md) adds deep bronze skin, high cheekbones, a narrow face and the shortest stature among the parents. Her white hair is genetic, beginning in early adulthood.

**Refined direction:** preserve the striking bronze/white contrast, narrow face and graceful presence. A later full-body key should make her petite stature and long musician's fingers legible. White hair must not imply an elderly face.

**Opening decision:** retain the current depiction. Her seated/leaning position is insufficient evidence of a height mismatch, and her hidden eyes do not justify an iris edit.

### Dorian

**Source:** `latest.md:37` establishes tall stature, broad shoulders, deep-set brown eyes, glasses and neatly trimmed mixed black/gray hair. [Dorian's biography](../../../wiki/bios/Dorian.md) adds rich dark skin, a wide nose and later accumulated aging/work details.

**Refined direction:** preserve the broad upper body, rich dark complexion, glasses and recognizable face. Later childhood references should retain modest visible gray alongside black hair. Maintain his animated storytelling gestures and attentive listening as separate performances.

**Opening decision:** preserve him for this test. Do not import the mostly silver beard, pronounced hunch or exact apparent age of his later biography into First Breath. Any hair adjustment should be its own small follow-up, assessed against the intended stage.

## What a dependable key still needs

The next visual keys should establish one face through front, three-quarter and profile views; full-body proportions relative to the cast; recognizable hair and complexion under neutral and warm lighting; a few natural expressions; and stage-specific changes. Compare Arin with Sage and Lyron with Kaleb in the same lighting and pose so costumes and props do not conceal similarity.

The familiars already have distinct species silhouettes and authored appearance guidance. This human-character trial does not propose changes to them. The childhood trio's earlier multi-view studies remain archived candidates; their existence is not evidence that all anatomy or identity problems were solved.
