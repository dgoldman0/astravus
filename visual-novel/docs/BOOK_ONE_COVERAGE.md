# Book I production-preview coverage

The preview boundary is **all of Book I: Seeds of Youth**, from the borrowed memory of First Breath through Joren's death and the ongoing acts of remembrance. It ends before **Book II: Growing Up**, whose opening explicitly moves into Calista's late teens. This is a kinetic adaptation: the events and their outcomes remain fixed.

The authority is [revision/latest.md](../../revision/latest.md), the aligned prose at commit `23f9766`. Line references below identify that text; passage names remain useful if later edits move the lines. The rough playable opening was preserved separately in commit `3dfcf6c`. Its five scenes did not constitute a complete source chapter or book.

This map distinguishes an essential development from a sentence-by-sentence transfer. Description can become an environment; repetitive routines can share a montage. An encounter, relationship change, revelation, or consequence still needs to occur in the playable story. Added connective dialogue does not become new canon in the prose or wiki.

## Chronology and reveal limits

- **First Breath** uses the newborn illustration and earlier-life parents. Kael is already older; Lyra's arrival comes later. The story gives no exact parental ages at this event.
- **Early family life** uses young Cali and young siblings. The montage shows practice developing across ordinary days. It does not attach an invented number of years to each lesson.
- **Early friendships and the established treehouse** remain childhood. Familiarity and accumulated possessions are already present in the prose's treehouse visit.
- **“As we grew older” at the water wheel** is the explicit visual-stage boundary within Book I. Use visibly older childhood designs and new clothing; do not advance to the late-teen designs that belong to Book II.
- **Joren's death and the first aftermath** use that later childhood stage. The prose does not specify an exact age or duration of mourning. Recurring anniversaries can be described retrospectively without showing an unchanged child at a claimed future year.
- **Living-world information** progresses from Lumen's parentage, to Sage's fictional Astravus, to the Tree of Echoes identification of Lumen. Title text, People descriptions, backgrounds and gallery thumbnails must obey that order too.
- **Transcendence versus death** becomes relevant through Cali's expectation of centuries together. The source's affectionate references to Joren's spirit do not establish a literal return or joining with Lumen, and do not need to be corrected in the characters' dialogue.

The [Astraviin lifecycle](../../wiki/worldbuilding/The-Astraviin.md) supplies broad development stages, not dates for these specific scenes. Later-life biography headings such as “125+” are not childhood-era parent ages. [Selene's white hair](../../wiki/bios/Selene.md) is genetic; removing it to make her younger would introduce another inconsistency. Scars, injuries or other biography traits with uncertain dates should not be assigned a new childhood origin by the adaptation.

## Dialogue and staging decisions

The family expansion keeps each parent's character while giving the child something immediate to do: arrange flowers, repair a mistake, find a note, follow a route, listen to a story, or help a frightened sibling. Lessons are carried by the interaction before the narrator names their meaning. This follows the project's [writing notes](WRITING_NOTES.md), rather than assuming that shorter dialogue alone is more natural.

The draft's repeated garden patience lesson, workshop learning, music practice and family meals appear as later variations in `family_rhythm`; the script does not repeat each introductory explanation. The first flute attempt and practiced melody use the same musical idea at different levels of fluency. Background changes place the montage in the garden, workshop, music room, library, central room and Sage's room.

New connective details include sorting screws into three trays, the particular flower-placement negotiation, the children's interruptions in Sage's story, and waiting for a second pot of soup. These make the source developments playable; they introduce no new historical events, family members or world mechanics. The siblings in Sage's tale are fictional characters told by Sage, not new residents of Lumen.

Environment art cannot show every hand movement. Actors are hidden for the flute performance, the close listening at the tree, the pond rescue, the lap-and-blanket bedtime story, and lantern release when a standing or held-prop sprite would contradict the action. Where an actor remains, clothing, age, mood and location must match the scene.
