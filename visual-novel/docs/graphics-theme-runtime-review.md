`scripts/capture_theme_art.py` run passed and captured all fifteen compositions
through Ren'Py's actual montage widget, at the middle of each cue, with playback
paused. Exact image hashes, cue positions and the cue-file hash are recorded in
`build/graphics-polish/theme-art-inputs.json`; screenshots are
`test-results/screenshots/theme-art-00.png` through `theme-art-14.png`.

the opening, Nibble, shared drawing and final outlook captures at native detail.
This checks the installed paintings after native fitting/cropping, supplementing
the individual source-art and support reviews. It does not certify animated
dissolves, real-time frame pacing, sound or the standalone MP4.
