## Art continuity

The plaza variants now share the great left tree, curved staircase, three-story residential terraces, central arched passage, right-side oval dais, paths and benches. A first anniversary candidate removed part of the upper central facade; it was rejected and regenerated from the memorial view. Festival lighting and decorations change the occasion while retaining that facade. Crowds extend through the foreground, square, balconies and connecting passage. The source gives no exact attendance number; the art does not establish one.

Reviewed the complete background and actor sets, including home/dusk, garden/treehouse exterior, dry/rain/remembrance interiors, character ages, familiar scale, and the mural's distinction from a live character. The principal corrections are the plaza, pond and waterwheel, construction light, missing supporting speakers, held lantern timing, and Cassia's shading. Existing approved treehouse architecture and familiar designs remain the references.

Cassia's young, older and mourning PNGs preserve her identity, underlying warm brown complexion, expressions, hairstyle, clothes and poses. The changes soften the lip boundary, recover facial midtones and give cheeks, nose and forehead more dimensional illumination. Runtime portraits crop those same corrected assets.
