recover off-screen floor plans, certify physical camera measurements, or make
cropped feet measurable. Close camera distance is not a change of age. Character
stage/identity uses the separate current source reviews and existing contracts.

landmark review. Cassia's comfort scene is receiving new furnishings and remains
open until that output and its runtime transition are inspected.

approve runtime cropping, UI overlays, film motion or audio.
