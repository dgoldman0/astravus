## Continuity decisions

- Preserve the explicit later-years ages: Calista is 125 and Elara is 75. Elara therefore takes First Breath when Calista is 50.
- Calista begins volunteering in her early twenties, meets Aris around 30, and undergoes Core Integration at 35 before their first mind-link.
- Kael forms his constellation shortly after Calista's integration. Nearly fifteen years of his parenting precede Elara's arrival. References to his eventual fifty-plus years of parenting describe the longer span of his life.
- Lyra is Calista's younger sister, born during Calista's childhood. She is a young adult awaiting integration when Calista demonstrates her new abilities.
- Elara remains an experienced adult after the 125/75 age anchor. Calista's transcendence develops after 125; its duration and final age remain unspecified.
- Aris uses he/him; Arin, Sage, Sol, and Lysandra use they/them. Existing she/her usage for Lyra and the other women is retained. Elara's childhood pronouns are retrospective, including before she states her preference.
- Lysandra's focus is mathematics. Their dedication to Calista is a formula, and their interest in musical patterns supports that focus. The former song event page remains as a redirect-style historical note.
- Calista has five parents. The dream of their first gathering includes all five and precedes their formal constellation. Arin is Elara's grandparent; Lyra is her aunt.
- Joren's death remains permanent. Shared dreams connect Calista with his parents and their memories; they do not restore Joren. Transcendence preserves personal awareness within an Astravus.
