# Elara's Light Sculpture Invention

## Overview

At 75, Elara is an established engineer developing interactive light sculptures. She demonstrates a prototype to Calista, who is 125, during an evening conversation in the garden.

## Technology and Demonstration

The device combines holography and kinetic elements with motion sensors and touch-sensitive surfaces. Shapes and colors respond to gestures: a flower can bloom, or a scene can change as someone moves a hand. Elara is experimenting with decoration, relaxation, storytelling, and education.

She brings the compact device from her bag and shows Calista several scenes. The demonstration prompts a conversation about the possibilities of her work. It remains in development; the draft does not depict a public launch or establish how widely the invention is adopted.

## Family Connections

Elara's engineering interests were nurtured by her grandparent Arin. Her work also reflects Calista and Aris's artistic influence. Later, she studies the adaptive sculpture brought home by her aunt Lyra and continues developing kinetic work with Arin.
