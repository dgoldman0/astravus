# Lysandra's Formula

## Overview
During their teenage relationship, Lysandra works out a complex formula and names it after Calista. This is the personal gift described in [the latest draft](../../revision/latest.md#book-ii-growing-up).

## Setting and Participants

- **Time:** Calista's late teens, before the breakup and first visit to Aurora.
- **Place:** Lumen; the draft does not specify the location of this moment.
- **Lysandra:** Mathematical theorist and pattern analyst, they/them.
- **Calista:** Their partner and the recipient of the dedication.

## Significance

The dedication gives Calista a joyful memory of their relationship. It shows Lysandra expressing affection through their own discipline. Their interest in the mathematics underlying music is compatible with this, but the draft does not depict them composing or performing a song as this gift.

The formula's content and formal name are unspecified. The event occurs during the relationship, rather than as a gesture following its end or following Calista's later partnership with Aris.
