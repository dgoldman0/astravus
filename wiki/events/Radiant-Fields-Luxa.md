# Radiant Fields and Luxa

## Overview

During her transcendence years, Calista visits the Radiant Fields, a new recreation district within Lumen. She watches Luxa, a sport Lyra encountered during her travels and brought into Lumen's cultural life.

## Setting

- **Time:** After Calista's 125th year, between periods of shared dreaming.
- **Place:** Lumen's Radiant Fields, including a large arena and a smaller practice field.
- **Participants:** Calista, players, spectators, and children practicing the game.

The Radiant Fields are part of Lumen. Luxa is the name of the sport; it is not a separate world or a pilgrimage destination.

## The Game

Luxa combines movement, dance, and strategy on a field with multiple levels. Glowing markers respond to players, whose movements leave trails of light. Teams capture glowing orbs and place them in their goals while avoiding opponents and using the terrain.

Calista sees a player catch an orb in the air and pass it to a teammate. Nearby, children practice the same movements on a smaller field. Their excitement connects her to the new activities developing around her.

## Significance

The visit shows Lumen continuing to grow while Calista approaches transcendence. Lyra's travels contribute to the community's culture, and younger residents make the imported game their own. This is a waking scene of communal recreation amid the shared dreams of Book IV.
