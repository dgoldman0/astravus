# Artificial Gravity

**Working canon, version 1 (September 2026).** See [Lumen's systems](Lumen-Systems.md) and [decision record](Lumen-Continuity.md). Artificial gravity is an accepted fictional capability of this setting. Its role is to support a consistent inhabited world; this page does not claim a demonstrated real-world mechanism.

## Ordinary habitation

Lumen maintains **nominal 1 g**, approximately 9.8 m/s², through connected homes, public routes, gardens, open inhabited volumes and the enclosed construction areas seen in Book I. Use ordinary-feeling weight, walking, climbing, falling water and animal movement in writing and animation.

Gravity acts throughout the relevant volume, including above the ground, and points in the same direction across connected habitation. A terrace is higher than the plaza; it does not have an independent down direction. Water cannot run uphill merely because two surfaces belong to different architectural elements.

The field does not require rotation or continuous thrust to provide everyday weight. Lumen maintains interior comfort during normal travel. Exact drive coupling and the limits of compensation during extreme manoeuvres remain open; ordinary gravity is not evidence that an arbitrarily violent acceleration is harmless.

## Control and variation

- Living structural and control tissues maintain the field as infrastructure, independently of whether a nearby inhabitant has undergone Core Integration.
- Environmental mood changes can alter light, warmth and sound without destabilizing gravity.
- Specialist enclosed volumes may deliberately use a different acceleration, including zero gravity. Their boundaries and transitions must be designed explicitly; they are not shortcuts through the ordinary walking network.
- Any future change of down direction requires a defined connection and transition. No current scene requires such a change.
- Luxa's multi-level playing field and graceful movement do not establish reduced gravity. Its default is the same as other inhabited areas.

## Boundaries and failure

Gravity coverage and air containment are different systems. An unpressurized exterior is not habitable merely because a field could be extended there. Covered gardens and unfinished internal domes remain inside the pressure boundary.

Treat normal service as highly reliable. A local malfunction is possible, but its mechanism, frequency and effects must be written for the specific event. There is no mandatory radiation burst, emotional feedback hazard or whole-vessel cascade in current canon. Nothing on this page assigns a cause to Joren's expedition accident.

Artificial gravity does not establish unlimited energy, faster-than-light communication, or a strong external gravitational attraction. Finite power, heat handling, structure and propulsion still need their own worldbuilding budgets.

## Previous Vacuum Stress Biasing proposal

The earlier page, [Artificial-gravitiy.md](Artificial-gravitiy.md), proposed **Vacuum Stress Biasing (VSB)** with a typical 0.1–0.4 g range, compulsory low/zero-g functional regions, and particular instability mechanisms. That proposal is superseded by the ordinary-habitation rules above. The original text is preserved in repository history, introduced at commit `bc8dcd4`.

VSB can remain a candidate in-universe technical term if developed later, but its vacuum-stress explanation is not established physics or current required canon. No precise mechanism is selected in version 1. This change follows the author's preference to accept artificial-gravity leeway while preserving the story and visuals.
