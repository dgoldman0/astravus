# Lumen: scale and physical foundations

**Proposed worksheet · 6 September 2026 · not a canon specification**

[Design study](../Lumen-Design-Study.md) · [Interactive review](review.html#scale) · [Reference atlas](Reference-Atlas.md)

## Population comes from the story's social scale

The manuscript establishes a young world, a majority from the first locally born generation, a wider community containing strangers, shared public ceremonies and growth across more than a century. It gives no census. A visible festival crowd is not a count of the population; neither a family's size nor an adult constellation supplies a household average.

Compare **6,000 and 18,000 embodied residents** at the childhood epoch. The author considers both reasonable possibilities for young Lumen, with **about 18,000 as the upper limit during Calista's childhood**. These remain alternatives, not a chosen census. The working 6,000–18,000 band allows distinct neighborhoods, specialized work and unfamiliar faces while keeping the shared community gathering in focus. This childhood limit does not determine Lumen's later population or a universal limit for other Astravii.

Count children and embodied adults, including residents needing care during gradual transcendence, in this population. Count visitors and familiars separately when calculating loads. Fully integrated minds require a separate, currently unknown substrate and resource budget; they cannot simply be counted as additional people occupying homes. A stable age distribution cannot be inferred from an assumed lifespan or founding year. Founding population, local births, immigration, departures and integration rates remain open. Keep later Calista-era growth as a separate epoch, not an automatic compound-growth forecast.

## An area budget without a single floor

These are **chosen design allowances**, not building standards or measured VN dimensions. Areas are usable surfaces distributed through the body, not the footprint under a dome. Keep categories separate: a productive bed within a public garden is counted once, and ordinary local circulation is included with its associated use. The dedicated transport allowance below is additional shared infrastructure.

| Use | Initial allowance, m² per embodied resident | Reason for reserving it |
|---|---:|---|
| Homes and their internal circulation | 60 | Private rooms and comfortable shared domestic spaces; no assumed household or partnership size. |
| Civic, learning, care, exchange and craft | 25 | Public gathering, schools, guild spaces, libraries, music and bodily care. Capacity of individual venues still needs a layout. |
| Public gardens and their paths | 140 | Substantial everyday planted space, shaded courts, heritage groves and ecological variety. This is not all food production. |
| Productive growing surface | 75 | A crop-based comparison for food systems; see research limits below. Growing surfaces may occupy multiple local levels. |
| Specialist services, processing and stores | 30 | Maintenance, recycling, food handling and reserves of material. Does not size propulsion or the whole pressure structure. |
| Shared transport interfaces | 20 | Lifts, interdistrict routes and freight handling beyond local paths. |
| Unallocated usable-space reserve | 40 | A visible allowance for omissions and future adaptation; not a prediction of later growth. |
| **Total** | **390** | **A sensitivity input, not an adopted density.** |

| Childhood probe | Total usable surfaces | Productive surface | Public gardens/paths | Net gathering area at 2 m²/person |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 6,000 residents | 2.34 km² | 0.45 km² | 0.84 km² | 12,000 m² |
| 18,000 residents | 7.02 km² | 1.35 km² | 2.52 km² | 36,000 m² |

The gathering allowance is a comparison, **not a safe crowd-capacity calculation**. It excludes stage, planting, route clearance and other unusable surface. The festival's visible galleries justify examining several connected occupied heights, but do not establish that thousands can fit. The actual court, approaches, visibility, acoustics and movement must be modeled before claiming the manuscript's gathering works at any of these populations. For scale, 36,000 m² is a 190 m square if flattened; that is an equivalence, not the proposed plaza shape. Reduce the census before enlarging the VN's intimate stage simply to accommodate an arbitrary target.

## Body dimensions and volume are a separate question

**Initial childhood search box:** 1.5–3 km length, 1–2 km width, 0.8–1.6 km depth. This is a compositional range to test, not a derived engineering requirement. It allows several neighborhoods and open views to recur along all three axes. It should contract if the local anatomy works in a smaller body; it should grow only for an identified spatial or physical need.

For a provisional ellipsoid, those two extreme envelopes enclose about **0.63 and 5.03 km³**. The actual silhouette, occupied fraction, tissue fraction and appendages are undecided. An ellipsoid is only a volume comparison, not the exterior design. Multiplying floor area by room height cannot determine these dimensions: rooms, shared tall air volumes, root beds, structural material, fluids, shielding, technical organs and unoccupied growth capacity all need their own accounting.

As a scale diagnostic, spreading 7.02 km² of usable surfaces through those envelopes gives roughly **90–716 m of envelope volume per square metre of usable surface**. That is not ceiling height. It shows how spacious even the small envelope could be, and how much of the larger envelope would need a reason to exist. Do not fill the unexplained volume with unspecified solid biomass or enlarge the outline to make a flat neighborhood seem three-dimensional. Sections must demonstrate distributed occupation and meaningful voids.

Start local modeling with ordinary rooms measured in metres, garden/court sequences measured in tens of metres and major neighborhood openings measured in hundreds. These scale families are hypotheses to test with recognizable doors, people, stairs, trees and camera views. Avoid making a familiar doorway enormous to reconcile background perspective.

For circulation, test local routes of roughly **100–500 m**, with longer travel using accessible lifts and collective transit. At a chosen walking speed of 1 m/s, 300 m takes five minutes before stops or vertical travel. A 2 km walk takes about 33 minutes. These are arithmetic probes, not journey times stated in the text. A child, a visitor and a resident without a Core must be able to reach ordinary destinations using physical interfaces. Continuous stairs, alternate paths and freight/service routes need space alongside faster systems.

## Physical foundations

| Status | Working treatment | Consequence for design |
|---|---|---|
| Accepted fictional allowance | Artificial gravity, with an initially common, approximately Earthlike down direction. Mechanism and exact strength remain open. | Familiar walking, water, hanging cloth, stairs and sports remain visually intelligible. No rotation-driven ring or low-gravity rewrite is required. |
| Established story property; mechanism open | A living Astravus and collective consciousness; responsive environment; gradual integration. | Lumen participates in daily life. A physical implementation of mind, its computational demands and any central organ are not established by this study. |
| Proposed far-future extrapolation | Engineered composite structure with living sensing, growth and maintenance, plus specialized technical systems. | Durable supports can carry loads while active tissues repair and adapt them. Craft, machinery and planned construction coexist with growth. |
| Retained physical constraints | Matter balance, finite energy, waste heat, pressure loads, material strength and transport time. | Reserve actual structure, stores, circulation and external heat rejection. Biological terminology does not eliminate their costs. |
| Not granted by the gravity allowance | FTL travel, instant long-distance communications, free energy, perfect recycling or invulnerability. | Leave propulsion, voyage conditions, communications and failure modes open until the story and research warrant decisions. |

### Cultivation and metabolic closure

NASA's controlled-environment work reports roughly 50 m² of crops per person for dietary calories under studied conditions, with strong dependence on lighting and cultivation. This is a useful order-of-magnitude comparator, not a complete diet, resilience or self-sufficiency guarantee. Our 75 m² allowance is an explicit exploratory choice; it is not a measured Lumen yield. Do not add separate oxygen-producing crop area blindly to the food allowance, or assume ornamental gardens can be credited at intensive-crop yields. [Wheeler, NASA, 2014](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20140017323).

ESA's MELiSSA work separates waste breakdown, nutrient conversion, photosynthetic production and the consuming organisms into interacting processes. That supports treating Lumen's metabolism as linked functions rather than one magical recycling room. It does not demonstrate a self-sustaining inhabited living vessel. Lumen's proposed system also needs buffers, trace-material accounting, contaminant management and repair access. [ESA, Closed Loop Compartments](https://www.esa.int/Enabling_Support/Space_Engineering_Technology/Melissa/Closed_Loop_Compartments).

A future combination of cultivation, fermentation and fabrication could change the crop-area comparison substantially. That remains a design choice requiring energy, feedstock and nutritional accounting. Automated routine production is already supported by the [socioeconomics article](../Socioeconomics.md); it should not force most inhabitants into manual subsistence farming or erase meaningful trade and creative work.

### Structure, air, water and light

Engineered living-material research has demonstrated tunable material properties and healing in specific experimental materials. It provides a research direction, not evidence that today's organisms can form a kilometre-scale pressure vessel. Lumen's load-bearing composites, long service life and coordinated repair are substantial fictional extrapolations. No strength, growth rate or radiation tolerance is assigned from this paper. [Manjula-Basavanna, Duraj-Thatte and Joshi, Nature Communications, 2024](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-53052-4).

Propose distributed structural supports and pressure-isolatable regions, with ordinary wide-open connections where conditions allow. The anatomy drawings are not a bulkhead plan: isolation boundaries, closure behavior and evacuation routes still need to be designed. Preserve physical access for inspection and repair; do not assume living systems prevent every accident. Joren's unspecified expedition malfunction remains unspecified.

Plant roots need soil or other substrate, water, gases and anchorage. A garden above a home therefore needs visible or concealed depth, drainage and load paths. Water descends under local gravity and needs energy to return uphill. Local rain is compatible with collection and humidity control; permanent rain throughout every region is not implied by the treehouse scene.

The manuscript's skylight and VN's artificial sky establish the lighting experience, not one transparent outer roof. Proposed light delivery and luminous architectural surfaces can reproduce a daylight cycle within deep regions; bioluminescence can supply intimate signals and accents. Keep heat and energy in the budget regardless of whether light arrives through optics or is produced locally. A real star-viewing deck requires an appropriate exterior-facing optical path; its later appearance does not require early homes to overlook space.

### Heat cannot disappear into the garden

Spacecraft thermal control balances absorbed and generated heat against storage and radiative loss. Radiator temperature, emissivity and exposure to a cold environment matter. For this study, use the idealized diagnostic `Q = ε σ A (T⁴ − T_sink⁴)` with ε = 0.9, negligible sink temperature and an unobstructed deep-space view. These are deliberately favorable assumptions; external illumination, shielding and mutual obstruction reduce net performance. [NASA, Thermal Control, section 7.1](https://www.nasa.gov/smallsat-institute/sst-soa/thermal-control/).

| Radiator temperature | Ideal emitted flux | Emitting area for 100 MW | Emitting area for 1 GW |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 300 K | 413 W/m² | 0.242 km² | 2.419 km² |
| 450 K | 2,093 W/m² | 0.048 km² | 0.478 km² |

These are calculations, not a reactor selection or total heat budget. Hotter radiators cannot passively cool cooler habitats to their operating temperature; moving that heat uphill requires work and adds heat. Count actual emitting surface with a useful space view, not the apparent outline of folded leaves. A living-looking radiator still has to function as a radiator; its visual form is undecided.

For sensitivity only, choose **100 W/m² of daily-average supplied power** for productive growing area. The two population probes then imply **45 MW at 6,000 residents** and **135 MW at 18,000 residents** for that input alone. This is not a horticultural equipment specification or a claim about conversion efficiency. Vary it in the review page. Much of supplied energy ultimately becomes heat; chemical storage and exports must be counted explicitly. Habitat lighting, embodied residents, the living body, integrated minds, industry, gravity and propulsion add unknown loads. No total power figure is ready for canon.

## What this study resolves, and what it leaves open

It supplies a volumetric direction, explicit comparative populations, a first area budget and a way to test heat consequences. It does not establish structural feasibility, life-support closure, full crowd capacity or VN camera fidelity. Before promoting numbers to canon, reconstruct the local section, map the important story routes and gathering spaces, allocate physical volumes and mass, then select power/propulsion assumptions with a defined operating context. Keep each later epoch separate. Record any required manuscript or asset change against the [compatibility register](../Lumen-Design-Study.md#compatibility-and-adjustment-register).
