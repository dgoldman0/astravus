# The Astravus Wiki

The wiki is the canon reference for the Astravus Collection: its people, living worlds, history, places and ways of life. It also describes the events and themes of Calista’s story.

Begin with the [setting](SETTING.md), meet the [characters](CHARACTERS.md), or follow the [timeline](TIMELINE.md). The story itself is in the [current manuscript](../revision/latest.md) and the [visual novel](../visual-novel/README.md).

## People and relationships

The [biography index](bios/INDEX.md) groups the cast by their relationships with Calista. Individual biographies describe their personalities, work, interests and lives across the story.

- [Calista](bios/Calista.md), her partner [Aris](bios/Aris.md), and their daughter [Elara](bios/Elara.md).
- Her childhood parents: [Maia](bios/Maia.md), [Arin](bios/Arin.md), [Selene](bios/Selene.md), [Dorian](bios/Dorian.md) and [Sage](bios/Sage.md).
- Her siblings [Kael](bios/Kael.md) and [Lyra](bios/Lyra.md), and Kael’s partner [Sol](bios/Sol.md).
- Her friends [Cassia](bios/Cassia.md) and [Joren](bios/Joren.md), her first love [Lysandra](bios/Lysandra.md), and the elder [Theron](bios/Theron.md).
- Her familiar [Shadow](bios/Shadow.md), and [Lumen](bios/Lumen.md) as a character.

A [constellation](worldbuilding/Constellations.md) is an adult romantic/peer relationship. Family and household describe other bonds and arrangements; children and familiars are not members of the adults’ constellation. The [character reference](CHARACTERS.md#continuity-reference) records pronouns and age relationships.

## Events and chronology

The [event index](events/INDEX.md) follows Calista from childhood through friendship, loss, love, parenthood and transcendence. Each event article describes its circumstances, participants and significance, with links to related moments.

The [timeline](TIMELINE.md) places those events within Calista’s life. It distinguishes approximate childhood periods from fixed milestones such as Core Integration at 35 and Elara’s First Breath when Calista is 50.
