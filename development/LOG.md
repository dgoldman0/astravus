## 6 September 2026 — Household connections and canon separation

The Book I source audit now supports a [local connection proposal](lumen/Lumen-Local-Connections.md) covering the named working rooms, all eight residents’ privacy, cooking and household facilities, Maia’s garden, both levels of the oak refuge, and routes into the wider community. Its reference tool accounts for all 45 inventory records and walks the 32 current VN scenes through proposed connections.

The proposal uses a connected home with a garden level and an upper private passage. Arin’s workshop and Dorian’s library are internally attached in this candidate; those attachments and the two-level arrangement remain design choices. Physical clearances, camera fit, crowd capacity and a measured 3D layout are still untested. No manuscript, VN narrative or visual asset was changed for this study.

The main proposal received a prose edit and is maintained separately from the browser generator. The source inventory’s long evidence tables can be expanded when needed. Generated labels and reference prose were reviewed for readability.

Design studies, audits, tools, revision plans and writing references moved out of the wiki into this development area. Canon articles retain the recovered household details. Prospective writing advice and explicit mechanism ideas are kept separately from the established setting. The old dialogue collection is preserved as a mixture of excerpts and expanded scenes, rather than presented as a transcript of the current manuscript.

Validation: all 15 source-register and route tests pass. Local document and section links resolve. Browser checks cover every space selector and scene-opening image, the 16 annotated reference plates, source-image loading, archived sketch notices and layouts at 390, 768 and 1480 pixels wide. The source fingerprints and generated outputs match the reviewed material. These checks establish reference consistency; physical geometry remains a separate task.
