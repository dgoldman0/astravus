# Development

This area holds work on the Astravus Collection: design proposals, source audits, writing references, ideas and development history. The [wiki](../wiki/INDEX.md) is the canon reference. A proposal or successful check here does not, by itself, establish a fact in the setting.

## Current Lumen work

The aim is to develop Lumen’s population, vessel size, inhabited regions and physical layout while preserving the current story. The VN provides the primary visual references; the manuscript and author direction supply further requirements.

- [Home, garden and neighborhood proposal](lumen/Lumen-Local-Connections.md): one possible arrangement of the complete childhood household and its surroundings. Room attachments and two local levels are proposed; dimensions and camera fit remain untested.
- [Interactive connection reference](lumen/lumen-study/connections.html): original VN images alongside the proposed arrangements, source passages and scene walkthroughs.
- [Book I source inventory](lumen/Lumen-Space-Inventory.md): the spaces, uses and connections found in Book I and all current VN scenes, with a local query tool.
- [Anatomy and scale study](lumen/Lumen-Design-Study.md): alternatives, population comparisons, physical assumptions and the adjustment register. Earlier incomplete local sketches are retained with withdrawal notices.

The childhood population comparison remains roughly 6,000 versus 18,000 residents, with about 18,000 as the author’s upper limit for that period. Artificial gravity is accepted. The body’s dimensions, later population and complete regional arrangement remain open.

The next spatial stage is to test the proposed connections against the VN cameras in a local 3D model, including all working rooms, personal spaces, household facilities, garden and both treehouse refuges. Books II–IV need a further spatial audit before the whole vessel is sized.

## Recording decisions

The [development log](LOG.md) records changes and their status. Keep alternatives, calculations, rejected approaches, test results and source audits here. Once a detail is settled, put a concise description of the resulting canon in the appropriate wiki article, with source references where useful. Keep the discussion and reasoning in development.

Both generated reference material and ordinary prose need an editorial pass for accuracy, wording, repetition and presentation. The main Lumen proposal is edited directly; its browser reference and searchable inventory use curated data. Read the actual sources and images when interpreting them.
