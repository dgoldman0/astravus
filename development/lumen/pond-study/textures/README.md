# Stone material

The three 2K JPG maps are from [Rock Boulder Dry on Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_boulder_dry), photographed by Dimitrios Savva and processed by Rico Cilliers. Poly Haven publishes this asset under [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license).

The diffuse, displacement and roughness maps supply surface detail on the modeled stones. They do not supply the paving layout or replace the VN reference. The Blender file packs these maps for portable viewing. Downloaded 8 September 2026 from the asset's `dl.polyhaven.org` distribution.
