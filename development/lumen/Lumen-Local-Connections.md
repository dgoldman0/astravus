# Lumen: home, garden and neighborhood

**Proposed arrangement · 6 September 2026. Room dimensions and the physical layout remain open.**

[Explore the proposal with the VN images](lumen-study/connections.html) · [Source inventory](Lumen-Space-Inventory.md) · [Design adjustment register](Lumen-Design-Study.md#compatibility-and-adjustment-register)

The working proposal is one home extending through two local levels of Lumen’s inhabited body. The garden level holds the central gathering room, cooking area, Arin’s workshop, Selene’s music room, Dorian’s library and Sage’s room. An internal passage rises to the children’s adjoining rooms and private retreats for Maia, Arin, Selene and Dorian. Outside, the household garden reaches the oak’s lower refuge; shared woodland continues beyond it.

Those attachments and the upper level are design choices. The story establishes the rooms and their uses but leaves much of their arrangement open. This proposal uses that freedom to preserve the current VN scenes. It does not adopt a new floor plan as canon or replace the withdrawn sketches with another measured layout.

## A home within Lumen’s body

The dwelling occupies the thickness of an inhabited branch or structural junction. Its garden-facing rooms open into a planted hollow. Quieter rooms continue behind and above them, with windows toward the light and greenery within Lumen. The household garden wraps around several parts of the home.

Other homes, gardens and passages continue through the surrounding body, including above and below parts of this setting. A path may pass through an enclosed stretch, emerge onto a planted ledge, then lead into broader woods. The [VN’s dome view](../../visual-novel/game/images/backgrounds/book-one/dome.png) guides this sense of depth: occupation and landscape recur throughout the view, with more of the living structure beyond it.

The close experience stays grounded in the VN’s familiar materials and activities: worn boards, curtains, tool drawers, soil, running water and unfinished work. Living murals, responsive light and planting carry Lumen’s presence into those spaces. The wider forest and open plains extend beyond this local study; their size cannot be inferred from the small garden around the oak.

## Shared life and working rooms

In the [central-room image](../../visual-novel/game/images/backgrounds/family-home.png), the passage beside the fountain can lead to the household halls, while the farther-right opening leads toward the garden. The proposed cooking area adjoins the gathering room beyond the existing camera view. The table, fountain, shelving and seating remain where the image places them.

Short hall branches connect the other rooms, with a route around the gathering area. Someone can carry supplies, go to bed or wash without crossing a painting session or a quiet conversation at the table. Meals, games, maps and painting continue to share that central room over time; nearby storage and drying space let unfinished work remain part of home life.

Arin’s workshop and Dorian’s library are attached internally in this candidate because their repeated use fits naturally into the family’s daily life. Their exact attachments are not stated in the source. If the later model needs a longer connecting passage to preserve a room’s appearance, that remains possible.

Cooking and household support also need actual space. The kitchen must allow Maia and Lyra to make the second pot of soup while the others remain at the table. Allow for food storage, bathing and toilets, clothes and linen, laundry and drying, garden handwashing, and storage for tools and art supplies. Distribute these around the household halls and garden entrance. Food preparation should have its own clean working surfaces; tool and paint cleanup belongs elsewhere. Room and fixture counts remain open.

The prose gives every resident a private space and places Kael’s and Lyra’s rooms on either side of Cali’s. The upper passage retains that relationship. Each room opens onto the passage, so no one’s retreat becomes a route through the home.

| Resident | Personal space in this proposal |

The four additional adult retreats are a proposed arrangement, not a room count supplied by the story. Their furnishings and sleeping arrangements remain open. This gives each adult privacy without treating their occupation as their entire personal life. It also leaves Sage’s established combination of rest and family company intact. A constellation does not determine these architectural choices.

The internal rise should be near the quiet rooms, keeping the return from Sage’s bedtime stories short. Both ordinary stairs and an accessible way between levels need room. The mechanism and exact path remain to be designed, along with washing and clothing storage on the upper level.

## The garden and the oak

Maia’s garden remains ordinary semi-private household space. It connects to the gathering room, cooking area and workshop passage, giving outdoor work and leisure a place in daily home life. Its cultivated paths lead to the oak at the wooded edge.

The [wide garden image](../../visual-novel/game/images/backgrounds/garden.png) already establishes the oak’s lower sitting platform, hollow entrance, ladder, upper landing and room. Keep all of them usable. An invited approach from the surrounding woods lets friends meet at the refuge without walking through the home. The shared through-path passes around the platform, leaving its cushions, tables and stored treasures undisturbed.

Two views constrain this arrangement: Cali can see the ladder from her planting patch, and the upper treehouse looks onto flowers and glowing garden paths. The planting close-up may face away from the oak, but the physical view from the patch must still exist. Planting and the garden boundary must fit around those relationships.

The recurring VN basin serves the planting, rescue and waterwheel scenes. Its broad dry bank needs space for pots, two children kneeling to help Lyra, and the embrace afterward. A wider path leads back to the garden entrance and washing area, allowing Lyra to walk beside Cali while holding her hand. Further garden water remains to be placed beyond those frames to support the prose’s references to multiple ponds.

The [waterwheel image](../../visual-novel/game/images/backgrounds/book-one/waterwheel.png) already shows a feed spout. Retain that small arrangement and connect it to the garden’s water circulation. The new requirement is a workable carrying route from Arin’s bench and enough dry space to settle the supports.

The mural occupies a garden-facing wall beside a household path, with room for painting, supplies and sitting. Preserve its [VN composition](../../visual-novel/game/images/backgrounds/book-one/memory-mural-v2.png), including the nearby water and planted approach. Family members and invited friends can pass through this space while it retains its intimate character.

Below and around the garden, the oak’s roots, growing substrate, pond water and drainage require real depth. Other rooms and paths can continue around that volume and emerge at different heights. The model must allow for these roots and supports before filling the space beneath the garden with habitation.

## Connections beyond home

Shared routes branch toward other households, the storytelling courtyard, woodlands and wider community places. They can split, rise through the body and rejoin elsewhere. The Central Plaza is one significant destination within this network.

Cassia’s home retains its craft and tea room, drying drawings, water feature and the room visible beyond the arch. Joren’s home retains Soren’s separate workshop, attached through a household work passage in this candidate. Both homes also need their own private rooms, cooking, washing, storage and outdoor life when developed further. Their sizes and arrangements remain specific to the people who live there.

The Tree of Echoes occupies a separate clearing reached through thicket. Farther along other routes, the construction region contains branching unfinished passages, its machinery room and the scaffold climb to the dome platform. Keep those destinations distinct. The background reused for Kaleb’s walk does not fix every outing to the same path.

The Central Plaza retains its stage, galleries and several approaches. Performing, floral displays, markets and remembrance require access for setup and storage. Its ability to accommodate the whole community remains unresolved at both childhood population options, roughly 6,000 and 18,000 residents. No capacity is inferred from the galleries merely being visible.

Shared guild workshops, studios, archives, training, exchanges, professional care and the Sanctuary remain part of the wider study. They need space in addition to the household rooms. Their distribution and childhood extent are still open, as are the larger transport, cultivation and maintenance systems. Later-book locations require a further source audit before vessel-wide dimensions are settled.

## What remains to test

The next model needs to demonstrate that these connections fit the existing spaces:

- Preserve the home’s camera composition while fitting every named room, each resident’s retreat and the practical facilities around it.
- Test the waterwheel’s carrying route and the wider path home from the pond, including turns and changes in level.
- Keep music audible in the hall and the fountain audible from Sage’s room, with meaningful privacy available in the home.
- Fit the planting-patch view of the ladder, assistance on the landing, both oak entrances and the upper garden view.
- Allow furniture and artwork to change through childhood within the same home, treehouse and plaza.
- Show enough root, water and structural depth, with other inhabited places continuing through a crossing section of Lumen’s body.
