# Visual reconstruction

The root `AGENTS.md` takes priority. These instructions add a workflow for the Lumen models in this folder.

- The current visual novel is the primary visual guide. Open the actual images for the scene and its other depicted states before constructing geometry. Read the accompanying scenes for the actions that the space must support.
- Establish the visible forms first: silhouette, enclosure, section, relative scale, openings, built-in furnishings, vegetation and the way ground meets water or structure. A list of matching objects does not establish a matching place. Generic room or landscape generators must not determine the architecture.
- Start with an individual recognizable scene. Compare its full render directly with the original image before expanding into surrounding rooms or paths. Inspect additional views and a section to expose geometry that only works from one camera.
- Distinguish observed proportions from chosen metre scale and unseen geometry. Keep uncertain measurements provisional. Fit connections around the reconstructed spaces; do not distort those spaces to satisfy an invented layout.
- Clearance tests, source fingerprints and projected landmarks answer limited questions. Review visual resemblance separately, and correct visible architectural differences before describing a model as validated.
- Rejected layouts and geometry remain reference material only. Do not inherit their dimensions or massing into a new attempt without independently establishing them from the sources.
