# Mentors Dinner Advice

*Writing reference from the [earlier dialogue collection](INDEX.md); may include expanded or rewritten material. Check the current story before treating added wording or staging as canon.*

## Context
Aris and Calista have dinner with Kael, Sage, and Sol—experienced parents who share their practical wisdom about raising a child. This scene provides grounded, real-world advice from family.

## Participants
- **Kael** - Calista's older brother, officiant for their ceremony
- **Sage** - Kael's partner, nurturing perspective
- **Sol** - Kael's partner
- **Aris** - Calista's partner
- **Calista** - Protagonist

## Dialogue

*One evening, they had dinner with Kael, Sage, and Sol, soaking in their practical advice. Their home was a blend of laughter and occasional chaos, a perfect example of a nurturing environment.*

**Kael:** "There's no one-size-fits-all approach to parenting. You'll find your way. Just be patient with yourselves and each other."

**Sage:** "And remember, it's okay to ask for help. We're all in this together."

*Seeing them handle conflicts and celebrate milestones gave Aris and Calista confidence.*

**Aris:** "We should have more family dinners like this. It's reassuring to see real-life examples."

## Constellation Ceremony

*Later, at their formal ceremony:*

**Kael:** "Cali and Aris, your commitment to each other and your future family is a testament to your love and dedication. This ceremony marks the beginning of your journey as a unified constellation."
