# Parents First Meeting Dream

*Writing reference from the [earlier dialogue collection](INDEX.md); may include expanded or rewritten material. Check the current story before treating added wording or staging as canon.*

## Context
During Calista's transcendence journey, she experiences a dream showing her parents meeting for the first time—a moment before she was born. This shared memory from the collective consciousness reveals the origin of her family.

## Participants
- **Maia** - Mother, at young age in the dream
- **Arin** - Parent, demonstrating technology
- **Selene** - Playing harp
- **Dorian** - Joining with a question about the device
- **Sage** - Joining the conversation
- **Calista** - Observer in the dream

## Dialogue

*The dream took Calista back to a time before she was born, where she saw her parents meeting for the first time. It was at a community celebration in Lumen's central plaza. The plaza was alive with music and laughter, bioluminescent plants casting a magical glow.*

*Arin was demonstrating a new piece of technology they had invented, while Selene played a melody on her harp that captivated the crowd. Maia, drawn by the music, wandered over and struck up a conversation with Arin.*

**Maia:** "It's amazing how your music and technology blend so seamlessly."

**Arin:** "It's all about finding harmony. Just like in nature, where everything has its place."

*Dorian joined them with a question about the device's history, and Sage drew another chair into the circle. Calista watched the five of them exchange stories as Selene rested her hands on the harp. This first gathering would eventually lead to their constellation.*
