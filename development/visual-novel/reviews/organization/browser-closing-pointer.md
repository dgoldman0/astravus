The failure was reproduced before changing the helper. First Replay left the pointer at CSS coordinates `(639.33, 428)`. Space and Escape returned to an end card with the Replay button at engine rectangle `[809, 613, 300, 58]`. The button belonged to the current screen and neither transition flag was active, but the focused displayable was `None`. A same-coordinate DOM `mousemove` appeared in the browser log while Ren'Py's `mouse_event_time` stayed unchanged. Moving two CSS pixels horizontally immediately produced focus on the current `Replay closing theme` button; returning to its center preserved that focus. The [failure](browser-closing-focus-failure.png), [restored hover](browser-closing-focus-restored.png) and [exact event/state observations](browser-closing-pointer-diagnosis.json) preserve this distinction.

Local Ren'Py 8.5.3 source inspection ruled out the alternative theory that `renpy_exec` restarts the interaction while `renpy_get` does not: `renpy-pre.js` implements `renpy_get` through `renpy_exec`; `core.py`'s `exec_js_cmd` uses `py_exec(cmd, hide=True)` without an interaction restart. `focus.py`'s mouse handler changes focus on engine mouse events. The observations locate the failure between same-coordinate DOM movement and engine mouse/focus handling, without claiming a more specific browser/SDL cause.
