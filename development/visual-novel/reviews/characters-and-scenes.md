## Findings and retained choices

- **Sage:** the standing sprite's iris rings read warm brown against the gray-eye biography and key. A narrowly masked native GIMP correction now makes them dark warm gray without changing pupils, catchlights, eyelids, face shape or body. The [authoritative refinement record](../art/characters/sage/iris-refinement/README.md) links the editable assembly, before/after source details, exact masks, pixel proof and fresh Ren'Py standing renders. It changes 77 source pixels and 56 rendered pixels; everything outside the source iris masks and all rendered alpha values remain exact. Native inspection shows a subtle pigment change with no luminous eyes or framing change.
- **Opening and parents:** Arin's pale freckled angular face and cropped auburn hair remain distinct from Sage's soft tan face and sandy sweep. The opening preserves five adult parents, their inward attention and the supported newborn. Selene's white hair remains a genetic identity trait rather than evidence of old age. Maia and Dorian retain distinct complexion, hair and facial structure. Modest stage variation does not warrant another face replacement.
- **Children and eye styling:** Cali, Cassia and Joren retain recognizable faces through their early/later outfits. Later figures have longer proportions, and frustrated or mourning variants change the expression appropriately. Lyra's revised green eyes remain intentionally wide and curious, with smaller stature than her siblings; the pond rescue, recovery and insect closeup retain fear, subdued aftershock and interest respectively. The stylized larger child eyes, Joren's bright grin and Cassia's expressive gaze are consistent design choices to retain. No further eye-size reduction is justified by this pass.
- **Anatomy and mood in CGs:** supporting hands and overlapping bodies read coherently in the rescue and embrace scenes; flute playing/rest, sketch holding, pointing, kneeling and hand contact communicate their activities. I found no concrete additional missing/extra-limb or hand repair worth a late repaint. Grief faces remain sober in the dim home and rain-lit treehouse. Warm memorial work and joyful closing memories preserve their different emotional purposes. Early memories within the closing montage do not reverse the continuous story's later-childhood progression.
- **Wardrobe and supporting adults:** Lyron's revised blue-gray overlapping woven garment retains his practical role and gentle face. Thalia, Soren and Kaleb remain distinguishable by face, hair and clothing. The author's retained wardrobes stay selected; this pass does not reopen the cultural design discussion or add arbitrary futuristic modifications.

The authority remains the [manuscript](../../../revision/latest.md), relevant biographies and [curated visual keys](../../../visual-novel/art/character-keys/index.html), read with the existing [continuity guidance](../../../visual-novel/docs/CHARACTER_CONTINUITY.md). In particular, [Sage's biography](../../../wiki/bios/Sage.md), line 11, specifies gray eyes whose shade varies with light. Later-life biography traits were not imposed on childhood.
