# Preview draft one — 0.1.3

This commit preserves the first rough playable draft before expansion to the full **Book I: Seeds of Youth** production preview.

The draft contains 153 dialogue blocks (1,655 words): First Breath, family orientation, the sunflower lesson, the two first friendships, and selected treehouse scenes. It does not cover the whole book. Missing material includes most individual family relationships, sibling experiences, the Tree of Echoes, the festival, the friends' families, later projects and conflicts, Joren's death, and remembrance.

Known presentation limitations: one standing design and outfit per speaking character, limited age progression, some visible sprite edge light, a short synthesized score and three environmental effects, and limited scene-specific emotional scoring. These are documented limitations of this baseline, not final design decisions.

The baseline passed Ren'Py lint, 26 native assertions, and a Chromium playthrough including save/load, cache refresh, and unsupported-WebGL fallback. Windows/Linux, macOS, and browser packages were built and checked; Windows and macOS runtimes were not executed on their target platforms.

Source, selected assets, build helpers, provenance and tests belong in version control. Downloaded SDKs, exports, ZIPs, caches, saves, test results and unused art remain ignored. Subsequent development should use focused, explained commits. The approved next scope is the complete first book, ending with Joren's loss and its lasting aftermath, without crossing into Book II.
