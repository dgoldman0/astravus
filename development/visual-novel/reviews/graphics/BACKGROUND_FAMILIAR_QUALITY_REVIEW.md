# Background and familiar quality review — 0.1-alpha

Reviewed September 5, 2026 by `matrix_release`: **26 backgrounds and three familiar portraits**, each opened at its full native resolution (1672×941 backgrounds; 1254×1254 familiars). The chosen comparisons are their selected Git blobs from `7b1c760`, recorded individually in the quality receipts. All 29 current files are byte-identical to those comparisons. That establishes absence of accumulated edit degradation; the visual findings below establish why the selected compositions remain suitable.

The review inspected full-frame focus and depth, foreground materials and sharpness, lighting, visible anatomy/identity, and recurring-location relationships. Empty foreground space is deliberate in backgrounds that receive runtime actors. It is not approval of an empty closing-theme montage. Human facial identity is not applicable to character-free rooms; incidental crowds are reviewed at their actual image scale rather than assigned unverifiable iris colors.
