# Remaining human source-art review

Root reviewed all selected human sprites in full-body and native-face comparison sheets, with a separate full-size inspection of gardening Maia. This record covers the 18 unchanged human images; the other eight repaired sprites have the independent review in [graphics-background-sprite-audit.md](graphics-background-sprite-audit.md). The checks are fresh observations, not inherited PASS labels.

Authority: manuscript and the physical-description sections for Calista, Cassia, Joren, Kael, Lyra, Maia, Arin, Selene, Dorian and Sage; supporting parents retain the established production designs where source descriptions are sparse. Childhood costumes and the existing relative-height contract identify stages without inventing numerical ages or centimetres.

Source-art findings support retaining identity, facial/body anatomy and expression, relative stage, painterly lighting/detail, and a usable dialogue pose. They do not imply that a neutral sprite literally enacts every narrated action. Scene-specific feet/contact and screen edges are runtime checks. Native framing was separately rerun: all26 silhouettes pass, wardrobe heights differ by at most3px and feet by at most2px. That technical result did not hide a visible Lyra hair-fringe problem, which is being handled as compositing work.
