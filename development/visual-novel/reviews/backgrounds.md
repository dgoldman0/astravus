The set deliberately ranges from luminous gardens to a very dim grief interior. The dark home remains orientable by its table lamp, door and established silhouette; uniformly brightening it would weaken that state. The populated plaza variants reuse substantial crowd staging, while their light and offerings carry the event change. No nearby figure presented an obvious scene-size anatomy defect during this view. Distant faces, fingers, small inscriptions, machinery internals and insect anatomy are too small to certify exhaustively.

This review covers complete source compositions, visible physical continuity and scene mood. It does not validate a dimensional building model, off-screen room connections, every prop’s mechanical operation, or current sprite/UI occlusion and accessibility modes. Native familiar placement/lighting is recorded separately in [familiar-compositing.md](familiar-compositing.md); release and browser framing checks belong to the integration pass.
