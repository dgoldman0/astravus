# Open mechanisms and facility ideas

These possibilities were embedded in the canon wiki. They remain available for development, while the wiki describes the established experiences and identifies what the story leaves unspecified.

## Technological character

The earlier technology article described an intention to keep the setting rooted in science fiction, with technology that can look fantastical while remaining explainable in principle. Current author direction develops that intention further: a living world whose people become part of it, with familiar craft and domestic life within a much more advanced civilization. Artificial gravity is an accepted fictional allowance. Specific mechanisms still need to earn their place through their consequences for the story and the physical world.

## Nanomachines and the Core

An earlier author note considered nanomachines permeating both the Astravus and its inhabitants, coordinating their systems. The former technology and Core Integration articles extended that idea to mental interfaces, transcendence and visible effects.

That is a possible implementation to investigate. The [technology article](../../wiki/worldbuilding/Technology.md) and [Core Integration article](../../wiki/worldbuilding/Core-Integration.md) describe the established capabilities without selecting it as the mechanism. Any later choice must account for the capabilities and limitations already shown in the story.

## Sanctuary spaces

The former wiki suggested separate preparation rooms, counseling spaces and gathering areas alongside private ceremony rooms. [Elara’s First Breath](../../revision/latest.md#starting-a-family) establishes a private room where her parents wait and a caregiver brings her to them. Preparation and counseling also occur in the story, but their complete arrangement within the Sanctuary is not mapped.

When developing this facility, determine how those activities share or occupy different spaces. The earlier room list is a starting question, not a settled plan.

## Life before First Breath

The earlier Sanctuary article raised possibilities involving development within the facility, a role for the Astravus in creating new life, different forms of genetic contribution, and advanced biotechnology. It also speculated that methods could vary between Astravii.

The story leaves those mechanisms open. Preserve its established preparation, caregiving and welcome while exploring them. The [canon Sanctuary article](../../wiki/worldbuilding/The-Sanctuary.md) records those known circumstances.

## Lyra’s later familiar

An earlier note suggested giving Lyra a new familiar as she grew older, possibly an owl. The old biography treated this as a future event, while the familiar article still quoted it as a possibility. It remains an idea to develop; no later companion is selected here. The [biography](../../wiki/bios/Lyra.md) retains her childhood bond with Nibble.
